A user in KEWEKE is a browser-based identity — not an email account. Your browser creates a personal key for you on first visit and remembers your display name. No passwords.

## What it is

- You pick a display name (1–48 characters). That name is what friends see next to items you add or edit.
- Behind the scenes that name is backed by a browser-generated key, so the service can verify that edits really come from you.
- Your avatar shape is derived from your identity plus your name — rename yourself and the shape updates.

## The three states

The settings page at `/user` shows which state you are in:

<UserStateBadgePreview />

- **Anonymous** — you have not chosen a name yet. You can still make lists; your items show as “unsigned.”
- **Local** — you have a name on this browser only. It appears on items you create here.
- **Remote** — your name is linked to the service. You can publish lists, edit shared lists, and approve your other browsers.

<UserSettingsPreview variant="remote" />

The table on that page adapts to your state — it shows only the rows that make sense for you.

## Where your name appears

Your name and avatar show up in places you already use:

<SignedBadgePreview />

- next to each item you add or last edited, in the shopping list
- in the header of the `/user` settings page
- in the small popup that explains who added an item

For a complete tour, see [What you see](/docs/keweke/users/what-you-see). How identities and live sharing work under the hood is covered in [Architecture — Identity](/docs/keweke/architecture/identity).

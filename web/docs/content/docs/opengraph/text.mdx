## Position & size

<PositionPreview />

Select a layer to see its position and size in **Properties → Position & size**:

- **X**, **Y** — position of the layer.
- **W**, **H** — size of the layer.
- **Rotation** — angle in degrees.
- **Opacity** — from `0` (transparent) to `1` (opaque).

## Typography

<TextPreview />

### Size and Weight

**Size** (in pixels) and **Weight** appear side by side. Weight ranges from **Regular 400** to **Extra bold 800**.

### Color

Below them is the **Color** field. Click the swatch to open the picker, then drag the field or type a hex value.

### Alignment

Three buttons control alignment:

- **Left** / **Center** / **Right** — the selected option is highlighted so you can see at a glance which is active.

### Font family

Above Size and Weight is the family picker. Built-in families are **Pretendard**, **Zilla Slab**, and **Google Sans Code**. Use **Upload font** to add your own WOFF2, OTF, or TTF file.
